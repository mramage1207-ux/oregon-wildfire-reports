Zone polygons: Oregon OEM Fire_Evacuation_Areas_Public. ZIP boundaries: U.S. Census Bureau TIGERweb ZCTA. All three layers can be toggled independently (top-right control). Zones and ZIPs let you visually check that a zone's shape actually falls within the ZIP code(s) it's credited to; "Unmatched zones" highlights zones whose addresses aren't fully reflected in the ZIP breakdown above (either no cached geometry at all, or only partial ZIP-boundary coverage) — the same shortfall the report's own "unmatched" summary row accounts for. ZIP codes near the fire-affected area are labeled directly on the map (and shaded even with zero evacuation addresses) so you can see exactly which ZIP an unmatched zone physically overlaps.
